About the festival

The entry point
to new cinema

Cyber Kino is a unique platform where the creative and technological worlds meet and cinema moves beyond conventional production. This is where the future of the industry takes shape, where new visual languages are born and formats appear that seemed impossible only a few years ago.

The core idea of the festival is to give talented people without large studios, multi-million budgets and huge teams a chance to enter the film industry through the new technologies that artificial intelligence provides. Today a single person with a strong idea can create projects that once required the resources of an entire film studio. Cyber Kino becomes the entry point for a new generation of directors, artists and digital creators.

Award categories

Six
directions

  • The best short films created with artificial intelligence technologies.

  • A commercial case: an advertising project for a brand or a product.

  • The best music videos and visual works created with generative AI.

  • Works that organically combine traditional live-action filming with the possibilities of AI.

  • Recognition of outstanding creative vision in working with AI tools.

  • A work created with AI tools alone — by one person, without a team.
