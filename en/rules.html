Submission 2026

Before submitting a film, please read these Rules and Conditions carefully. By submitting your film to the Cyber Kino (ISFF) competition, you clearly understand and agree to these Rules and Conditions.

Entry requirements

  1. We accept international short films created with the use of artificial intelligence.
  2. Entries are submitted by email to cyberfilm.fest@gmail.com, stating:
    • Email subject: Submission 2026
    • Film title (in English, and in Russian if that is the original language)
    • Name(s) of the film director(s)
    • A link to view the film. Please make sure the preview file plays correctly.
    • The preview file must be in English or have English subtitles.
    • Country of production
    • Running time of the film
    • Film category
    • Synopsis, promotional materials, press materials;
    • Film poster
    • Continuity or dialogue lists in the original language in electronic form and/or a subtitle file in Russian, English or the original language in .srt format;
    • Stills from the film in digital format (up to 10 items);
    • Materials for the catalogue (information about the film’s creators, photographs, including a photograph of the director).

If the film is selected

  • The information provided in the entry (synopsis, credits, etc.) will be used in marketing and promotional materials without changes.
  • Prepare the screening copy of the film for delivery starting from 1 August 2026.
  • The screening copy must be provided no later than four (4) weeks before the start of the festival.
  • The screening copy must be accompanied by English subtitles (as a separate file) and be identical to the preview version.
  • Films will be subtitled in the languages required for the screenings.
  • The festival has the right to use excerpts of selected films of no more than 10% or a maximum of 3 minutes of the total running time to promote the festival in various media, on the internet, as well as for archival and other non-commercial purposes.
  • The festival provides one representative of the film with free access to all screenings at all PLAYGROUNDS* venues. More detailed information about hosting guests will be provided to selected participants.
  • The festival does not pay screening fees for films

Conditions of participation

  1. Participants are responsible for obtaining the rights and permissions from the owner of the copyright and other rights related to their short film.
  2. If the Organiser has reasonable doubts as to whether a competition work meets the requirements of the selected category — including where there are signs of the use of generative artificial intelligence technologies in categories where this is prohibited or restricted — the Organiser has the right to request additional materials from the Participant confirming the process by which the work was created. The Participant undertakes to provide the requested materials within the period set by the Organiser. If such materials are not provided, or if the materials provided do not make it possible to confirm that the work meets the requirements of the competition, the Organiser has the right to refuse admission of the work or to withdraw it from the competition. The decision of the festival management is final.
  3. The Cyber Kino festival accepts entries in the following categories:
    • Short films created entirely with AI technology
    • Hybrid-format short films created partly with AI technology (no less than 30% of the material)
    • Experimental short films created entirely with AI technology
  4. Each short film must be submitted to the competition in one of the categories listed above. However, in some cases the selection committee reserves the right to place a film in another category or a special programme.
  5. The official language of the festival is English. Films in other languages (including Russian) are accepted only with English subtitles.
  6. We accept short films in any genre. For example: Absurdist, Action, Adaptation, Adventure, Advertising, Art-house, Biography, Black comedy, Classic, Children’s film, Comedy, Crime, Dance film, Detective, Drama, Cult film, Epic, Family film, Fantasy, Gangster film, Horror, Film noir, Historical film, Magical realism, Musical, Mystery, Melodrama, Road movie, Philosophical film, Romance, Satire, Science fiction, Silent film, Slice of life, Social film, Sports film, Superhero film, Supernatural film, Surrealism, Suspense, Thriller, Urban film, Western, Zombie film, etc.
  7. The running time of films submitted to the competition must be no less than 1 minute and no more than 30 minutes.
  8. Films must be completed after 1 January 2025.
  9. We do not accept unfinished copies of films.

Evaluation process

All films submitted to the competition will be watched in full by at least two judges. They will be considered and evaluated according to criteria such as the quality of the script (plot, dialogue, etc.), acting, production quality, originality, technical level, cinematography, sound design, editing, the location or locations used and the art design.

Notification

Directors whose films have been accepted for the competition (“Official Selection”) will be sent notifications by email no later than 3 weeks before the Festival takes place. The list of accepted films will also be published on our website. The list of winners (“Winners”) will be published no later than one week after the official selection notification has been sent.

Screenings

The selected films (“Official Selection” and “Winners”) will be published on our website.

  • We do not screen all submitted films, only those highly rated by the Jury.
  • The main venue of our events is in Moscow; the final venues are confirmed no later than 3 weeks before the Event takes place.
  • Winners are included in the festival’s competition programme.

We may also screen short films that did not receive an award but made a deep impression on our selection committee.

Miscellaneous

  1. To take part in the Cyber Kino competition, the screening of a film does not have to be a premiere. Short films that have already been shown at other festivals are also eligible.
  2. The Cyber Kino competition has no requirements relating to the public screening of a film or its release online. If your short film has been published on Vimeo, etc., it is still eligible.
  3. Changes to submitted films are allowed only in exceptional cases agreed with the Festival team.
  4. The decision of the selection committee is final.
  5. Stills from submitted films may be published on the festival website, as well as informational and promotional materials, including press releases, festival brochures and other materials.
  6. Cyber Kino reserves the right to update or change these rules and conditions without prior notice.
  7. Once the selection is complete, notification letters will be sent by email.
  8. Cyber Kino does not accept trailers or rough cuts. Please bear in mind that members of the Cyber Kino selection committee will not watch multiple versions of your film when considering it for the programme.
  9. Cyber Kino (ISFF) is granted the right to use excerpts from any submitted film for the purpose of promoting the festival. The person or company that submitted the film hereby warrants that they have the right to submit this film for screening and that they understand and accept these requirements, rules and regulations.
  10. Cyber Kino (ISFF) reserves the right to reject entries.
  11. The screening copy must be in English or have English subtitles.
  12. The festival’s programme directors may select and invite projects on their own (without an entry fee). Invited authors will be notified by email or telephone.
  13. The applicant is responsible for observing all copyrights. By submitting a film, you undertake to provide it for screening if selected, subject to confirmation of participation by the director.
  14. The applicant warrants that they hold all the necessary rights, licences and permissions to submit and screen the film. By submitting a film, the applicant agrees that, if selected, the film will be provided for screening at the festival, and this obligation takes effect once confirmed by the director. By submitting a film, the applicant confirms and warrants that they have obtained all the necessary permissions from the director and, on the director’s behalf, agrees that, if selected, the film will be provided for screening at the festival.

Legal matters

The persons or organisations submitting a film for participation are responsible for securing all rights to the content of the work related to the use of the film within the framework of the festival. By sending their film, they confirm that they are its authorised representatives and that the screening or other use of the film by the Festival will not infringe the rights of third parties (for example, holders of copyright, image rights or trademark rights), and that they have obtained all rights to any materials used in the film that were produced by other parties (music, footage, photographs, text, etc.). In addition, they guarantee to protect Cyber Kino ISFF from any third-party claims related to the screening/use of the film within the framework of the Festival, including covering any possible legal costs.
