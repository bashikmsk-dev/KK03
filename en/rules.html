1. General provisions

1.1.The Cyber Kino international festival of short works created with the use of artificial intelligence (hereinafter — the Festival) is held by the sole proprietor Alexander Olegovich Bashilov, PSRNSP (ОГРНИП) 319774600098385, TIN (ИНН) 772372464850 (hereinafter — the Organiser).

1.2.The competition programme of the Festival is a public competition under Chapter 57 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation. These Regulations are its conditions.

1.3.Terms. In the Regulations, terms are used in the meanings given below. Terms that are not in this section are understood in the meaning established by the legislation of the Russian Federation, and where the law does not define them, in the meaning commonly accepted in film and video production. Terms written with a capital letter (Work, Applicant, Organiser and others) have the meaning given in this section throughout the text.

3. Who may take part

3.1.In 2026 participation is possible only from the age of 18. The Applicant may be an individual who has reached the age of 18 on the date of the entry — the author of the Work or an authorised representative of the rights holder.

3.2.If a Work is submitted by a company, the entry is submitted by one named individual — its representative. They are responsible for the accuracy of the entry on an equal footing with the company.

3.3.The Organiser, its staff, the members of the jury and their close relatives may not take part. Works in the creation of which they took part are not accepted, nor are Works of companies they own or run.

3.4.If a member of the jury is directly or indirectly connected with a Work, its authors or the applicant company, they do not evaluate that Work (clause 11.6).

3.5.One participant or one team may submit no more than 3 Works. Each Work is submitted as a separate entry.

4. Categories

4.1.A Work is entered into one of the categories of the main competition. The requirement for the use of AI is set out in clause 4.3.

CategoryWhat is acceptedRunning timeUse of AI
AI Short Film A short film from 1 to 30 minutes 100% AI in the image
AI Advertising An advertising spot for a brand or a product, under the rules of clause 4.5 from 10 seconds to 30 minutes 100% AI in the image
AI Music Video A music video or a visual work for music; the rights to the music are required from 1 to 30 minutes 100% AI in the image
Hybrid format (AI + live action) A Work combining live action and material created by AI from 1 to 30 minutes AI combined with live action, the share of AI is at least 30%

4.2.Special categories. The jury may award them to Works of the main competition; no separate entry is submitted:

  • Best AI Director — for outstanding creative vision in working with AI tools;
  • Sovereign — a Work created by one person without a team and entirely with the help of AI: not only the image, but also the basis of the script, the music, the sound, the voices and other elements. To grant it, the jury may request the materials of clauses 7.3–7.4.

4.3.“100% AI” means that the entire image of the Work — every frame — is created by generative AI models, without live action, stock video or photographs. The requirement concerns the image only: the editing, colour grading, titles, script, sound and music may be produced by any means. The exception is the special category “Sovereign” (clause 4.2). In the hybrid format the share of AI must be at least 30% of the material.

4.4.The selection committee may move a Work to another category or to a special programme, having notified the Applicant.

4.5.Special conditions of the AI Advertising category.

4.5.1.Three kinds of Works are accepted:

  • commissioned — created on the order of a real advertiser, released or not;
  • spec works with a real brand — created without a commission, only with the written permission of the brand owner;
  • for a fictional brand — without permission, provided the brand is not confusingly similar to an existing trademark.

4.5.2.When submitting an entry, the Applicant is obliged to make sure that they have obtained from the brand owner or the advertiser written permission for the Work to take part in competitions and festivals, for its public screening and for its use in the materials of the Festival under section 9. The entry states the brand, the advertiser and the contact details of their authorised representative. Submitting an entry constitutes a warranty that such permission exists.

4.5.3.A copy of the permission is provided within 5 days of the request. The Organiser may contact the advertiser to verify it. If the advertiser does not confirm the permission, the Work is withdrawn from the competition.

4.5.4.A spec Work with a real brand contains in its end title or description the note: “This work was created for a competition and is not official advertising of [brand]”, unless the brand owner has permitted otherwise.

4.5.5.A Work is submitted in the form in which it was created or released. Campaign results (reach, sales, awards) are stated only where they are accurate and can be confirmed by the advertiser.

4.5.6.Works advertising goods and services whose advertising is prohibited or restricted in the Russian Federation (art. 7 of Federal Law 38-FZ “On Advertising”: including tobacco and nicotine-containing products, unlicensed gambling, means of bypassing blocks — VPN) are not accepted, nor are Works withdrawn by the advertiser or found to be improper advertising, nor Works that pass off an AI image as real footage of events, people or results (clause 7.11).

4.5.7.Works in this category are shown at the Festival. The Organiser may choose not to publish advertising Works on the website and in social media, or to agree such publication with the advertiser.

5. Requirements for the Work

5.1.The running time is as set out in the table in clause 4.1, including the titles.

5.2.The Work was completed no earlier than 1 January 2025.

5.3.The final version of the Work is provided with the entry. The selection is carried out on it, and it is also the screening copy. Trailers, rough cuts and unfinished versions are not accepted. The jury watches one version of the Work.

5.4.The screening copy is provided in MP4 or MOV format, at a resolution of up to 2K (2048×1080). The link must allow the file to be downloaded.

5.5.Works not in English, including those in Russian, are accepted only with English subtitles — burned in or as a separate .srt file. Subtitles in other languages needed for the screenings are prepared by the Organiser.

5.6.Premiere status is not required. Works shown at other festivals or published on the internet are accepted.

5.7.Replacing the submitted version is allowed only in exceptional cases, as agreed with the Organiser.

5.8.Works of any genre are accepted, provided their content does not breach section 10.

6. Submitting an entry

6.1.An entry is submitted through the form on the “Regulations” page of the Festival website. The fallback route is an email to submit@cyber-kino-festival.com with the subject “Submission 2026” and the same details.

6.2.The entry contains:

  • the title of the Work in English and in the original language;
  • the name of the director (directors);
  • the name of the Applicant, the Applicant’s country, a contact e-mail;
  • the country of production, the running time (min:sec), the category;
  • a link to the final version of the Work and the password to it, if there is one;
  • a link to the materials in a single folder: the poster, up to 10 frames, .srt subtitles, information about the creators, a photograph of the director;
  • the synopsis;
  • the age rating of the Work: 16+ or 18+ (18+ if it contains obscene language or other information prohibited for children);
  • for the AI Advertising category — the brand, the advertiser and the contact details of their representative (clause 4.5.2);
  • separate confirmations of acceptance of the Regulations, of consent to the processing of personal data and of consent to its publication (section 16).

6.3.The links and files must remain available and unchanged until 31 December 2026 and must not contain malware.

6.4.A notification of the outcome of the entry is sent to the Applicant’s e-mail address within the time limits set out in section 2.

6.5.Incomplete entries may be rejected. The Organiser may request the missing details and materials.

7. Use of artificial intelligence

7.1.A Work must meet the requirement for the use of AI of its category (clauses 4.1, 4.3). The Applicant may use any AI models, services and platforms.

7.2.The Applicant complies with the terms of use, the licence conditions and the rules on the use of results of every AI service with the help of which the Work was created.

7.3.AI declaration on request. It is not submitted together with the entry. If a Work raises doubts among the members of the jury that it was created with the use of AI in accordance with the requirements of its category, the Organiser sends the Applicant a request. In reply, the Applicant states:

  1. all the AI models, services and platforms and their versions, the account under which each tool was operated, and the share of the Work created by each of them;
  2. the source and the licence of every element created without AI: music, sound effects, voice, fonts, stock video and images, own footage;
  3. for every face, voice, likeness or performance in the Work — whether it is synthetic or based on a real person; and, if on a real person, confirmation of their consent.

7.4.Verification of authenticity and of the creation process. The Organiser may, at any time before the Winners are announced and, for awarded Works, afterwards as well, request:

  • edit projects and timelines (collects, EDL, XML), source files;
  • prompts, including those for key frames and characters;
  • the generation history and generation variants, including unused ones;
  • shot logs, references and other materials reasonably necessary to verify the authenticity of the AI generations, the creation process, originality, the share of AI and the rights to the materials.

7.5.The materials under clause 7.4 are provided within 5 days of the request. The Applicant keeps them for at least 24 months after the end of the Festival. Failure to provide the materials is grounds to withdraw the Work from the competition.

7.6.The Organiser may check the Work for originality, plagiarism, similarity to other works, signs of AI and infringement of copyright, including by means of third-party services. By submitting an entry, the Applicant consents to such checks.

7.7.It is prohibited to remove, alter, conceal or circumvent watermarks, provenance data and labelling applied by AI tools.

7.8.Rights to AI results. The Applicant understands that copyright protection of materials created by AI differs from country to country. Under the law of the Russian Federation, only a citizen who created a result by their creative labour is recognised as its author (arts. 1228, 1257 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation). Rights to the results of AI work may be determined by the terms of the tool’s provider. The Organiser does not guarantee that any element of the Work is protected by law or free from claims.

7.9.The Applicant is themselves responsible for ensuring that the terms of every AI service permit the use of the Work under the Regulations — public screening and promotion — and, where necessary, takes out a commercial subscription.

7.10.Real people, voices and deepfakes. The Applicant is obliged to hold the signed consent of every recognisable real person whose image, voice, name, performance or likeness is present in the Work or has been used for the training, prompting or fine-tuning of any tool (art. 152.1 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation).

7.11.It is prohibited:

  • to depict, imitate or clone the voice of a real person in a deceptive or defamatory manner, or without documented consent;
  • to use deepfakes of a real person, living or deceased, without consent;
  • to create synthetic images of real people and events that could be taken for genuine footage;
  • to use the image of a celebrity, a public figure or a brand in a way that creates the impression of their endorsement. The exception is Works in the AI Advertising category submitted under the rules of clause 4.5; images of real people in them are permitted only with their consent.

7.12.Failure to meet the requirement for the use of AI, an inaccurate AI declaration, failure to provide the materials under clause 7.4, removal of labelling or a breach of clauses 7.10–7.11 are grounds for withdrawing the Work from the competition and revoking an award (section 15).

8. Rights to the Work and the Applicant’s warranties

8.1.The Applicant warrants that they:

  • are the author of the Work or are authorised by the rights holder to submit it and to permit its screening at the Festival;
  • where the Work has several authors (a team), have obtained the consent of all co-authors to participation and to the licence under section 9 (art. 1258 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation);
  • where the Work was created under an employment contract, on commission or as part of studies, have obtained the consent of the employer, the client or the educational institution (art. 1295 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation);
  • have obtained the director’s permission, if they are submitting the Work not on the director’s behalf;
  • hold written licences for all third-party materials: music, footage, photographs, texts, fonts, stock — with the right of public screening;
  • the Work does not infringe the rights of third parties to their image, trademarks or other rights;
  • the Work is not bound by exclusive contracts that prevent its participation.

8.2.The Applicant is obliged to inform the Organiser immediately if a warranty has ceased to be true or if a claim has been brought in respect of the Work.

8.3.The Organiser does not acquire the exclusive right to the Work. The right to conclude a contract on the use of the Work under art. 1060 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation is not granted to the Organiser.

8.4.The same Work may not be submitted several times — by one Applicant or by different Applicants, including into different categories. Repeated and nearly identical entries may be rejected.

8.5.The Organiser makes no claim to the ideas and concepts of Works. A similarity of ideas or plots between different participants does not in itself mean an infringement of rights and does not give grounds for claims against the Organiser.

9. Licence to the Festival

9.1.By submitting an entry, the Applicant grants the Organiser, free of charge, a simple (non-exclusive) licence for the territory of the whole world. The term of the licence is 3 years from the date of the entry and, for the archive and for materials already published, the whole term of the exclusive right (art. 1235 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation). The ways of use are:

  • for all submitted Works — reproduction for selection and evaluation;
  • for shortlisted Works — public screening at the Festival; use of frames, the poster and fragments no longer than 10% of the running time and no more than 3 minutes to promote the Festival in various media and on the internet, as well as for archival and other non-commercial purposes; the creation of subtitles in the required languages;
  • for the archive — keeping a copy of the Work, frames and the description in the Festival archive, and preserving materials already published.

9.2.The details from the entry (synopsis, titles, information about the creators) are used in the materials of the Festival without changes to their substance.

9.3.The Organiser does not sell the Work and does not transfer it to third parties for a fee without a separate contract with the Applicant.

9.4.The Festival pays no remuneration for the screening of Works. The Applicant retains the right to distribute the Work freely.

9.5.When a Work is screened at the Festival and listed in the programme, its title and the name of the author (the director) are stated. In showreels, montage clips and trailers composed of several Works, a general list of the Works and authors in the end titles or in the description of the publication is sufficient. Adding subtitles and the Festival logo and changing the file format are not considered an alteration of the Work, and the Applicant consents to them.

9.6.After the end of the Festival the Applicant may ask in writing that no new publications of fragments be made. Materials already published and the archive are not deleted.

11. The jury and the selection

11.1.The line-up of the jury is published on the Festival website. The Organiser may change the line-up of the jury during the entry period, having notified the Applicants by e-mail and on the website. A change in the line-up of the jury does not change the criteria or the procedure of evaluation.

11.2.The jury watches and evaluates the Works throughout the entry period, as they arrive. Every admitted Work is watched in full by at least two members of the jury.

11.3.The stages of the selection:

  1. checking the entry for compliance with the Regulations, including section 10;
  2. the viewing of the Work by at least two members of the jury and the decision to include it in the shortlist — the notification is sent as the Works are watched, no later than 4 December 2026;
  3. publication of the Festival programme — 4 December 2026;
  4. determination of the Winners — the announcement and the awards ceremony at the Festival on 11–13 December 2026.

11.4.Works submitted in the final days of the entry period are watched from 28 November to 4 December 2026 on the same conditions. The absence of a notification by 4 December 2026 means that the Work has not been shortlisted.

11.5.The criteria of evaluation:

CriterionWeight
Idea, script and dramaturgy30%
Use of AI: the meaningfulness and originality of the techniques25%
Visual solution and creativity25%
Technical execution: editing, sound, sound design20%

For the AI Advertising category, the criterion “Idea, script and dramaturgy” is assessed as the idea and its fit with the brand’s task.

11.6.A member of the jury does not evaluate a Work with which they are directly or indirectly connected: where its authors, the applicant or the client are their relatives, friends, colleagues, employer, employees or a company they work with. They inform the Organiser of such a connection, and the Work is evaluated by other members of the jury.

11.7.The decisions of the jury are final. The Organiser is not obliged to disclose the scores or to explain the decisions.

11.8.The programme directors may invite a Work to take part. An invited Work submits an entry and goes through the selection on general terms.

WhoAward

13.1.All notifications are sent to the e-mail address given in the entry. The Applicant keeps an eye on the spam folder. A notification is deemed received on the day it is sent.

13.2.Shortlist notifications are sent as the Works are watched by at least two members of the jury, no later than 4 December 2026. The list of shortlisted Works is published on the website together with the programme.

13.3.The shortlist determines the composition of the screening programme. Inclusion in the shortlist does not mean that an award has been granted.

13.4.A shortlist participant confirms their participation within 2 days of the notification. Without confirmation the Organiser may exclude the Work from the programme.

13.5.The Festival programme is published on the website on 4 December 2026.

13.6.A participant may announce publicly that they have been shortlisted as soon as they are notified, and may use the digital laurel they have received. The Organiser asks that the official accounts of the Festival be tagged in such publications. Announcing a win before the official announcement at the Festival is not allowed.

14.1.The Festival takes place on 11–13 December 2026 in Moscow. The address of the venue is announced on the website no later than 4 December 2026.

14.2.Shortlisted Works are screened. The Organiser may also screen out of competition other Works that impressed the jury.

14.3.The final version submitted with the entry is used for the screening (clause 5.3). The Organiser may request the file in a screening format; it is provided no later than 7 December 2026.

14.4.The Festival is included in the list of film festivals of the Ministry of Culture of Russia. Each Work is screened at the Festival no more than 5 times.

14.5.Sessions are held with an age rating (clause 10.4).

15. Withdrawal from the competition and revocation of an award

15.1.The Organiser may refuse admission, withdraw a Work from the competition or revoke an award at any stage if:

  • the Regulations have been breached, or a warranty or the AI declaration is inaccurate;
  • the Work does not meet the requirement for the use of AI of its category;
  • plagiarism, infringement of the rights of third parties or a well-founded claim by a rights holder has been established;
  • the requirements of section 10 have been breached;
  • the advertiser has not confirmed the permission (clause 4.5.3);
  • the Applicant attempted to influence the jury or impersonated another person.

15.2.A Work whose award has been revoked or which has been excluded from the shortlist loses the right to the diploma and the laurel. The Organiser publishes a corrected list on the website.

15.3.An Applicant may withdraw their entry by email before the programme is published. Materials already published are not deleted.
